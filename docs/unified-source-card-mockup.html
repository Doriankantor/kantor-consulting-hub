Each axis at each surface, from the live code. The pattern is clean: Intel is the content workbench (edits geography, actors, tags, confidence, relevance), New Sources is the routing desk (edits sections and incident), All Sources is the archive (edits nothing). That single-dimension difference is what makes one shared card honest rather than a flag-riddled mega-component.
